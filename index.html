Privacy Policy — WonderKids Learning

An offline educational app for children aged about 3 to 8. Published by Lunavox. Last updated: October 2026.

No ads No tracking Stored on device

1. Who we are

WonderKids Learning is an offline educational app for children aged about 3 to 8, developed and published by Lunavox. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Lunavox. You can reach us at the contact address at the end of this policy.

2. What we store on your device

The app keeps the following on this device only, in a private database and private preferences file that other apps cannot read:

  • The first name you type for a child profile (you may leave it blank, in which case it defaults to "Explorer")
  • The avatar emoji you pick
  • Stars, coins and daily streak
  • Per-world progress and per-skill mastery scores
  • Your sound, vibration, guide-voice and screen-time-limit settings
  • A flag recording whether the All-Access Pass is active

This information is never uploaded to us and never sent to any server we control.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, photographs, precise location, contacts, files, calendar data or health data. We do not read device identifiers such as IMEI, MAC address or the Android advertising ID, and we include no advertising or analytics SDK.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional All-Access Pass is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. To confirm and restore your subscription the app sends the purchase token that Google Play gives it back to Google Play, and keeps a local flag recording whether the pass is active. Google's own privacy policy governs the payment itself.

The Google Play Billing library that handles this purchase includes Google's own Play Services components and its DataTransport reporting pipeline, which send billing events to Google. We never receive or store that information. The billing library also declares a location component and a place-reporting component; we have deliberately removed both, because this app does not use location in any way and asks for no location permission.

5. Sharing a certificate

The app can create an achievement certificate. If you tap Share, Android opens the share sheet and sends the text of the certificate — which includes the child's profile name — to whichever app you choose. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share, and we never receive a copy.

6. Advertising and tracking

WonderKids Learning contains no advertising, no behavioural advertising networks, no affiliate trackers and no tracking cookies. This applies to the free version as well as to the All-Access Pass.

7. Voice and sound

Spoken guidance uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device, and sound effects are generated on the device. The app does not record audio and has no microphone permission.

8. Parental gate

The grown-ups area is protected by a maths question that must be answered by typing the result. There is no skip button. This keeps children out of the parent settings and away from the purchase screen.

9. Your controls

You can delete any child profile, together with that child's progress, at any time from the Parent Dashboard. Deleting every profile removes all learning data the app has stored. You can also erase everything at once by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

10. Children's privacy

WonderKids Learning is directed at children, so we keep data collection to the minimum described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the optional first name stored on the device, and we do not transmit any of it. We aim to meet the requirements of the Google Play Families policy and of children's data protection laws such as COPPA. If you believe a child's information has reached us in error, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

All learning data is held in the app's private storage area. Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so this data is not copied to your Google Drive and cannot be extracted with adb backup.

12. How long we keep data

We keep nothing, because we receive nothing. On your device, data stays until you delete the profile, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the version shown in the app and in the Play Store listing, and revise the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact us

Questions about privacy or child safety are welcome. Lunavox is the data controller for this app.
